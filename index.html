Ask a question
Which mail accounts can I connect?

Leqa supports Gmail, QQ Mail, and self-hosted Exchange through EWS and NTLM. Server policies can affect access. Gmail and QQ password connections require an app-specific password or authorization code rather than your regular account password.

Does my email pass through a Leqa server?

Leqa connects directly to your mail provider. Optional third-party AI, sender images, remote mail images, iCloud organization sync and a configured push relay have their own data flows. The privacy policy explains each of these choices.

Does local archiving free my Exchange space?

A local copy keeps the server original and does not reduce your quota. Migration verifies the saved local mail before removing the server copy. Your archive lives on that device, so keep separate backups of anything important.

Is there another AI subscription to buy?

AI writing is included with Leqa Pro, but a third-party AI provider may charge separately. Connect your own supported account or API key. On-device Apple features require compatible hardware and supported languages and regions.

Where can I download Leqa?

We’re preparing the App Store release. This page will link to the listing when it is available. Leqa is currently built for iPad in landscape, with English and Simplified Chinese interfaces.
