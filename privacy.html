Leqa Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Maintained by the Leqa development team

Leqa is an email client. This policy describes the current app’s handling of mail, optional connected features and purchases. Your email account remains with your email provider; Leqa does not create a separate hosted email account for you.

Email and authentication

When you connect an account, Leqa communicates with your chosen Exchange, Gmail or IMAP/SMTP service to read, synchronize, search and send mail and perform the actions you request. Mail content, attachments, addresses and account details are processed for those features. Passwords, app passwords, OAuth credentials and AI API credentials are stored using Apple Keychain. They are used to authenticate to the corresponding service. The developer does not operate a mail proxy that receives your mailbox or password.

Information on your device

Mail caches, downloaded attachments, drafts, an outgoing-mail queue, signatures, historical recipient suggestions, local archives, preferences and chosen profile images are stored locally as needed. The mailbox cache and local archive records use the app’s device-local encryption; some preview files, exported files and image caches are separate files. Device data protection and any backups you enable also affect their protection and retention. Do not treat an exported mail or attachment as encrypted by Leqa.

Local archives and deletion

Copying mail into a local folder keeps an independent local copy. A migration can also remove the server copy when you explicitly choose that operation. Clearing downloadable caches does not delete local archives. You can remove connected accounts, delete local folders and their messages, clear caches, or remove the app. Exported files and copies in Files, Photos, backups, other apps or your mail provider remain under your control and must be deleted separately. Removing an account does not delete the provider’s account or cancel a subscription.

External images and avatars

Displaying remote email images makes requests to their original hosts, which may receive your IP address and other ordinary network metadata; some messages include tracking images. Use the app’s remote-image controls to limit this. If real sender avatars are enabled, Exchange photos are requested from the connected Exchange server; other avatars may use the sender’s domain website or an email-address hash sent to Gravatar. These services apply their own privacy policies. You can use built-in avatars instead.

Translation and AI

The built-in translation feature uses Apple’s Translation framework and may require language downloads. Available Apple Intelligence writing and search features use Apple’s system model. Optional connected AI features send the text or mail context needed for your request to the AI service and endpoint you configure, using your own account or API credential. That provider’s processing, retention, cross-border transfer and training terms apply. Do not enable a provider for confidential company mail unless you are authorized to share that content with it. Leqa’s developer does not operate an intermediary AI server.

Optional iCloud and notifications

If configured and enabled, iCloud key-value synchronization stores mail-organization states such as completed, snoozed and category choices with hashed message identifiers in your Apple iCloud account; this feature does not upload mailbox credentials or email bodies. Disabling sync stops future synchronization and does not automatically erase existing iCloud records. You may manage iCloud data through your Apple Account settings. Notifications use system permission and mail synchronization. An optional push relay, if you configure and register one, receives an installation identifier, app identifier and APNs device token. Mail credentials are not included in that registration. The relay operator’s policy applies.

Purchases and Family Sharing

Apple handles Leqa Pro payments, subscription billing, free-trial eligibility, refunds and Family Sharing. Leqa reads product information and verified transaction/entitlement records to unlock features. The app does not receive your complete payment-card or bank-account details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted local email archives.

Permissions and sharing

Profile photos and inserted pictures can be chosen with the system photo picker. Exporting, printing, previewing or sharing mail uses the action and destination you select. Adding calendar invitations uses calendar permission where required. These operations may create copies outside Leqa. You can revoke available permissions in system Settings.

Developer collection, retention and contact

The current app does not contain third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs and does not sell your mailbox data. Apple may separately provide purchase reports and diagnostics according to its settings and policies. Local technical diagnostics may record operation status to help troubleshoot the app. If you send a support report or attach a log, the material you choose to disclose is received through that support channel and used to resolve the issue. Avoid including message bodies, passwords, tokens, bank details or private addresses in public reports.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you have voluntarily shared with the developer, open a minimal request at Leqa Support. Do not post sensitive information publicly. The app is a general-purpose email tool and is not specifically directed to children. The policy will be updated when relevant functionality or data practices change, with a revised effective date.

Leqa 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日 · 维护方：Leqa 开发团队

Leqa 是邮件客户端。本政策说明当前 App 如何处理邮件、可选连接功能和购买信息。你的邮箱账号仍由邮箱服务商提供，Leqa 不另行创建托管邮箱账号。

邮件与身份验证

连接账号后，Leqa 直接访问你选择的 Exchange、Gmail 或 IMAP/SMTP 服务，以读取、同步、搜索、发送邮件及执行你要求的操作。这些功能会处理邮件正文、附件、地址和账号信息。密码、专用密码、OAuth 凭证和 AI API 凭证使用 Apple 钥匙串保存，用于对应服务的认证。开发者没有运营接收你邮箱或密码的邮件代理服务器。

本机数据

邮件缓存、下载附件、草稿、发件队列、签名、历史收件人建议、本地归档、偏好设置和选择的个人头像按功能需要保存在设备上。邮箱缓存和本地归档记录使用本机密钥加密；部分预览文件、导出文件和图片缓存为独立文件，其保护及保留还受设备数据保护和你启用的备份影响。导出的邮件和附件不应被视为已经由 Leqa 加密。

本地归档与删除

复制到本地文件夹会保留独立副本；明确选择迁移时，也可能删除服务器副本。清除可重新下载的缓存不会删除本地归档。你可以移除账号、删除本地文件夹和邮件、清除缓存或卸载 App。已导出至文件、照片、备份、其他 App 或邮件服务商的副本需分别管理和删除。移除账号不会注销服务商的账号，也不会取消订阅。

外部图片和头像

显示邮件远程图片会访问图片原始服务器，对方可能接收 IP 地址等常规网络信息；部分邮件含跟踪图片。你可以使用远程图片控制来限制加载。启用真实发件人头像时，Exchange 照片从已连接的 Exchange 服务器获取；其他头像可能访问发件人域名网站，或将邮箱地址的哈希值发送到 Gravatar 查询。对应服务适用其自身的隐私政策。你也可以改用内置头像。

翻译和 AI

内置翻译使用 Apple Translation 框架，可能需要下载语言包。可用的 Apple Intelligence 写作和搜索使用 Apple 系统模型。可选的联网 AI 功能会将完成请求所需的文本或邮件上下文发送至你配置的服务和接口，并使用你自己的账号或 API 凭证。该服务商的数据处理、保留、跨境传输和训练条款适用。未经授权，不应将公司机密邮件交给外部 AI 服务。Leqa 开发者没有运营中间 AI 服务器。

可选 iCloud 与通知

配置并启用后，iCloud 键值同步会在你的 Apple iCloud 账号中保存完成、稍后、分类等整理状态及哈希邮件标识，不通过该功能上传邮箱凭证或邮件正文。关闭同步会停止后续同步，不自动删除已有云端记录；你可通过 Apple 账号设置管理 iCloud 数据。通知使用系统授权和邮件同步。若你自行配置并注册推送中继，中继会收到安装标识、App 标识和 APNs 设备令牌；注册不包含邮箱凭证，中继运营者的政策适用。

购买与家人共享

Apple 处理 Leqa Pro 付款、订阅扣费、试用资格、退款及家人共享。Leqa 读取商品信息及已验证交易和权益记录以解锁功能，不接收完整银行卡或银行账号信息。订阅可在 Apple 账号设置中管理或取消；恢复购买不会恢复已删除的本地邮件归档。

权限与分享

个人头像和插入图片可由系统照片选择器选取。导出、打印、预览、分享邮件会使用你选择的操作及目标。添加日历邀请会在需要时请求日历权限。这些操作可能在 Leqa 以外生成副本，你可在系统设置中撤销可用权限。

开发者数据处理、保留及联系

当前 App 不含第三方广告或行为分析 SDK，不出售邮箱数据。Apple 可能根据其政策和设置提供购买报表或诊断。本地技术诊断可能记录操作状态以帮助排错；若你主动发送支持报告或日志，其中你选择提供的信息会通过该渠道用于解决问题。请勿在公开报告中附上邮件正文、密码、令牌、银行信息或私人地址。

隐私咨询或请求删除你主动提供给开发者的信息，可通过 Leqa 支持提交简要请求。请勿公开敏感信息。本 App 是通用邮件工具，并非专门面向儿童。功能或数据处理方式变化时，本政策将更新并注明新的生效日期。
